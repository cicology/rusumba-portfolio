The project

Manual grading does not scale for South African schools, colleges, and universities. But the obvious fix, AI that grades for you, runs into a harder problem: a teacher will not trust a mark they cannot see the reasoning behind.

Cleverly is built around that tension. The goal was never to replace the teacher, but to do the heavy lifting and then show its work.

What I did

  • Founded the company and set the technical direction as Cofounder & CTO, owning architecture, AI systems, and cloud infrastructure.
  • Built a vendor-neutral AI provider layer so the grading engine is not locked to a single LLM, models can be swapped or tiered by cost and task.
  • Designed evidence-linked, rubric-aligned grading with a teacher-in-the-loop review step, so every mark traces back to the rubric and the student''s own work.
  • Built a tiered OCR pipeline that balances cost against handwriting-recognition accuracy, routing harder scripts to stronger models only when needed.

Where it landed

Live in pilot with South African educational institutions. Prompt caching and model tiering keep the unit economics sustainable as usage scales, the difference between a demo and a business.
