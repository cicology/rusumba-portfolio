The project

123tutors matches students with tutors. The platform had outgrown the no-code foundation it launched on, and at the same time students needed more support than a tutor roster alone could provide, help at 11pm the night before an exam, when no tutor is online.

I returned as CTO to solve both at once: move the platform onto a foundation it could grow on, and give students an AI tutor that is always available.

What I did

  • Led a full platform migration from Bubble to Next.js/TypeScript on Prisma and Supabase, without losing the existing user base or data.
  • Shipped a Claude-powered AI Tutor via AWS Bedrock, with real-time streaming chat and intent classification.
  • Built generated interactive mindmaps so the AI Tutor can lay a concept out visually, not just in text.
  • Set the technical roadmap now extending into the platform''s matching engine and institutional reporting.

Where it landed

Migration live, AI Tutor shipped and in daily use by students. The platform now runs on a stack the team can extend, instead of one they had to work around.
