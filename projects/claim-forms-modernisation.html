Claim Forms Modernisation

RoleArchitect & Contributor (team)
ClientInsurTech group (internal)
Year2025
StatusRolled out
Node.jsPDF ParsingE-SignatureBrowser-NativeReact

The project

Hundreds of claims forms were trapped in a deprecated, Adobe-only PDF format. A customer on Chrome, Safari, or a phone hit a dead end, they had to install desktop software just to file a claim. In an insurance context, that is lost claims and frustrated customers.

The job was to free those forms from Adobe entirely, and to replace a paid third-party signing tool while we were at it.

What I did

  • Helped architect an in-browser pipeline, with the Alpha engineering team, that reads each form''s own underlying blueprint and reconstructs it as a fully interactive web form.
  • Preserved conditional logic, calculations, and draw-to-sign, then exported a pixel-accurate PDF on submission, identical to the original on paper.
  • Built an in-house document execution and e-signature platform that replaced a paid third-party subscription entirely.
  • Rolled it out across the full form library, working end-to-end from browse to signed, submitted PDF.

Where it landed

Rolled out across the full form library, working end-to-end from any browser to a signed, submitted PDF. The e-signature platform is live handling agreement workflows, and the Adobe dependency is gone.
