The project

University campuses run on cash and cards that vendors cannot always process, and students already carry a physical ID card that does nothing beyond proving who they are. uTap turns that card into a payment method and the phone into the terminal.

I joined as an engineer with equity in the venture, owning core technical pieces of the build.

What I did

  • Worked across a multi-app ecosystem: a React Native/Expo student app, a vendor dashboard, and an admin portal.
  • Led the full payment gateway migration onto Paystack across mobile, web, and backend.
  • Contributed to the NFC tap-to-pay work, the piece that lets a student tap to pay with their student card.
  • Took iOS NFC as far as the platform currently allows, with support in progress pending permissions.

Where it landed

Preparing for pilot launch with an initial university partner, with iOS NFC support in progress. The student app, vendor dashboard, and admin portal are built and integrated on Paystack.
