The Operations Layer

RoleArchitect, Builder & Contributor (team)
ClientInsurTech group (internal)
Year2025
StatusIn production
ReactNode.jsAWS LambdaEventBridgeSES/SNSMicrosoft Graph

The project

Three very different businesses, insurance, hospitality, fitness, each running on manual processes that did not scale: paper-heavy underwriting, phone-in reservations, and monthly billing done by hand.

The work was to replace those manual processes with systems, across all four businesses, alongside the Alpha engineering team.

What I did

  • Insurance: CRM systems for underwriting, claims, and broker management; customer-facing quote calculators and policy portals; a broker onboarding workflow; and REST APIs integrating underwriting partners, banks, and reinsurers.
  • Hospitality: a real-time booking and capacity-management system for a high-traffic rooftop restaurant.
  • Fitness: a membership platform plus an automated recurring debit-order run, scheduled batch billing with bank reconciliation, on Lambda, EventBridge, S3, and SES/SNS.
  • Internal: an HR portal with org-chart, employee self-service, and a wellness booking system, integrated with Microsoft Graph.

Where it landed

All in production. Policy processing time is down 60% and manual data-entry errors down 85%, the measurable result of turning manual workflows into systems.
