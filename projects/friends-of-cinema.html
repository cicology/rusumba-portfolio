Friends of Cinema

RoleSole Builder
ClientIndependent film production company
Year2026
StatusLive in production
ReactViteTypeScriptFramer MotionSupabase

The project

An independent film production company needed a website that felt like the films they make, art-house, editorial, cinematic, not another templated agency homepage. For a creative studio, the site is a portfolio piece in its own right.

I built it solo, front to back, with the visual craft the brief demanded.

What I did

  • Built the full React/Vite/TypeScript site end to end, solo.
  • Crafted the motion and feel, Framer Motion page transitions, a custom cursor with mix-blend-mode effects, and a full-screen parallax hero with a film-grain overlay.
  • Built a filterable portfolio grid for their film work.
  • Backed it with Supabase (Postgres, Storage, Auth) on a client-manageable content schema with RLS policies, so the studio can update it themselves.

Where it landed

Live in production on Vercel, a site that carries the studio''s creative identity rather than flattening it into a template.
