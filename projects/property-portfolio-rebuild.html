Property Portfolio Rebuild

RoleArchitect & Contributor (team)
ClientProperty development company
Year2025
StatusIn final QA
Next.jsReactContent ModelingMigration

The project

A property development company''s public site was running on a five-year-old template-based stack. Every content update was a risk, and the underlying framework was long past its supportable life, the kind of technical debt that quietly gets more expensive every month.

The work was a full rebuild that modernised the foundation without changing what the public and existing clients already relied on.

What I did

  • Helped architect a full rebuild, with the team, onto a modern Next.js architecture.
  • Migrated every property listing, gallery, and page into structured, maintainable data models.
  • Preserved exact public-facing content and behaviour, so nothing clients depended on shifted underneath them.
  • Verified page-by-page against the live site for full content and visual parity ahead of cutover.

Where it landed

In final QA, verified page-by-page against the live production site for full content and visual parity ahead of cutover, a modern, maintainable foundation replacing an unsupportable one.
